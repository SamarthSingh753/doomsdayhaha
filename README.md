# Marvel Multiverse: Road to Doomsday — Master MCU Binge Checklist 🛡️⚡

An ultra-sleek, reactive, and gamified Marvel Multiverse & MCU binge checklist built to prepare for *Avengers: Doomsday*.

Contains all **115 Marvel & Multiverse titles** in your exact watch order, spanning:
- **MCU Sacred Timeline**: Phase 1 through Phase 6
- **The Defenders Saga**: Daredevil, Jessica Jones, Luke Cage, Iron Fist, Punisher, and The Defenders
- **Spider-Man & Sony's Spider-Verse**: Tobey Maguire, Andrew Garfield, Tom Holland, Miles Morales, Venom, Kraven & Madame Web
- **Fox X-Men Universe**: The entire Mutant timeline, Wolverine trilogy, and Deadpool saga
- **Fantastic Four**: 2005, 2007, 2015, and *The Fantastic Four: First Steps* (2025)
- **Marvel Legacy & Animation**: Blade, 2003 Daredevil, Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D., Agent Carter, X-Men '97, What If?, Eyes of Wakanda, and more!

---

## ✨ Features

- **⏳ Real-Time Countdown to Doomsday (December 18, 2026)**:
  - Live ticking countdown display with Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds.
  - Dynamic **Marathon Pacing Calculator**: computes the exact number of titles per week you need to watch to finish before the premiere!
- **🏰 Official Poster-Inspired Theme (12.18.26 IS DOOMSDAY)**:
  - Marvel Studios official red badge and chiseled metallic "AVENGERS" wordmark with toxic emerald underglow.
  - Neon green "DOOMSDAY" typographic treatment featuring the sharp Avengers emblem.
  - Atmospheric Latverian gothic stained-glass tracery backdrop and warm torchlight amber aura inspired by the official posters.
- **🖼️ 4 Interactive View Modes with High-Res Posters**:
  - **List View (Default)**: Sleek horizontal rows displaying official movie/series poster thumbnails (56×84px), titles, saga tags, star ratings, and personal notes.
  - **Compact List**: Ultra-dense single-row list (34×50px mini posters) designed for fast binge scanning across all 115 titles.
  - **Cards Grid**: Showcase poster art banner with synopsis preview and quick-check controls.
  - **Detailed View**: Cinematic widescreen view featuring large posters (120×180px), full synopsis, runtime, star ratings, and inline note previews.
- **🎯 "Up Next In Your Marathon" Spotlight**:
  - Automatically identifies and highlights the next unwatched movie or show in your list with poster art.
  - 1-click "Mark Watched" shortcut.
- **⚡ Filter & Search**:
  - Filter tabs by Multiverse saga / era with live counter badges.
  - Status filters: All / Unwatched / Watched.
  - Format filters: All / Movies / TV Series / Specials & Animation.
  - Real-time search with instant matching.
  - Sort by: Watchlist Order, Release Year, or Alphabetical.
- **🎨 Doom's Obsidian Glassmorphism**:
  - 4 Dynamic theme palettes: **Doctor Doom Emerald**, **Arc Reactor Cyan**, **Stark Crimson**, and **Vibranium Violet**.
  - Interactive starfield particle canvas with twinkling cosmic nebula.
  - Checkmark celebration micro-animations and confetti bursts.
  - Synthesized Web Audio API sound effects (with mute toggle).
- **📝 Personal Ratings & Notes**:
  - Rate any watched movie/show 1 to 5 stars.
  - Add your personal reviews, memorable quotes, or favorite moments.
- **💾 Auto-Save & Export**:
  - Full automatic persistence with `localStorage`.
  - Export formatted checklist to clipboard or download/import JSON backup.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

### Option 1: Direct Open
Simply double-click [index.html](file:///d:/antigrav%20projects/doomsday/index.html) in your browser. No build step or installation required!

### Option 2: Local Dev Server
```bash
npm run dev
# or
npx serve . -l 3000
```
Then visit `http://localhost:3000` in your web browser.
